WHAT THIS IS ABOUT

ERA ORA — BEYOND THE BOOK is an exhibition about disruption in the world of work—how roles, identities, and everyday systems shift under technological change, and what gets reclassified as “normal” after the shock.

The experience is framed as a retrospective from the future: as if you are visiting the exhibition ten years from now, looking back on the present as the moment when the rules began to change. It’s a deliberate pretense—an archival stance—meant to make today feel observable, even strange, through the distance of time.
